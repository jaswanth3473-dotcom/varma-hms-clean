Patient Experience

From Booking to Recovery — Seamlessly Guided

Our digital system removes all friction — from booking to billing to follow-up care.

1
Book Online or by Voice
Choose department, doctor, and available slot in minutes. Confirmation email arrives instantly.
2
Arrive with Your Token
Your token number is generated on booking. No queuing, just walk in at your slot time.
3
Doctor Consultation
Doctor reviews your history, records diagnosis and generates a digital prescription instantly.
4
Pharmacy — Same Token
Prescription is automatically sent to the pharmacy. Collect medicines using your token number.
5
Follow-up & Records
Access all your records, prescriptions and appointment history from your patient portal anytime.
